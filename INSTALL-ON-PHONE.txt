YJ Studios — phone and direct portfolio uploads

1. Open the published website over HTTPS on your phone. Sign-in and uploads do not work when opening index.html from a files app.
2. Scroll to Studio Work and choose + Add Project. Sign in using the authorized Firebase account when prompted.
3. Select an audio, image, or video file from your device, complete the project details, preview it, then save as a draft or publish it.
4. Upload progress and status appear in the form. Published projects appear in Studio Work; edit, unpublish, and delete actions appear on project cards while signed in.

Firebase setup is required first: enable the selected Authentication provider, add the website domain to Authorized domains, grant the Firebase UID its trusted custom claim from a trusted environment, enable/initialize Storage on the approved billing plan, and deploy Firestore and Storage rules. See FIREBASE_SETUP.md for exact commands and limitations.

The service worker caches the public site only. Project management needs an internet connection. Do not publish admin-tools or credentials with the site.
